# Robustness to the latency model

Generated by `src/scripts/robustness.py`. Held-out workloads (five real traces, four held-out synthetic shapes), 20-minute warm-up, 5 seeds, every controller swept over target utilisation 0.4-0.95. The equal-CPU comparison of `results/REPORT_v5.md` is repeated under each model. CHIMERA was not re-tuned for any of them.

Every service-time distribution has mean 0.1 s of CPU. Slowdown laws: `inverse` 1/(1-u) (reported); `kingman` 1 + u/(2(1-u)); `steep` 1/(1-u^4).
